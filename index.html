01 — A little context

Still curious.
Always becoming.

My interest in computers started around Grade 8. By Grade 9, I had made my first webpage — a small beginning that opened up a much bigger world.

Through Grades 9 and 10, I kept building pages, websites and web apps while learning HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Now, alongside my studies, I’m exploring AI tools and agents and finding new ways to bring ideas to life.

I’m drawn to the mix of logic and imagination: a well-made interface, a sketch taking shape, or a science project that makes you ask one more question.
